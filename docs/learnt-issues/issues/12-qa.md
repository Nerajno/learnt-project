## Scope

Run this final pass in **both themes** on Home, Archive, a Composted detail page, a Shipped detail page, About, Contact and 404.

- **WCAG 2.2 AA:**
  - text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 at 24px and up);
  - visible focus;
  - targets at least 44px (24px minimum per 2.5.8);
  - logical heading order;
  - landmarks;
  - `aria-current` and `aria-pressed` correct;
  - stage meaning never carried by colour alone.
- **Reduced motion:** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; drop the card lift on hover.

- **Theme:** the system preference is respected on first visit; the choice persists; no flash on reload.
- **Lighthouse:** Performance and Accessibility at 95 or above; SEO 100.
- **Other:** OG/Twitter meta on every page, the sitemap, and a styled 404 page.
